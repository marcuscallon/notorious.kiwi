heading: *Product,* finance, *architecture* & operations.

## 01 :: Product / CPO :: feature
### Customer-problem-solution fit *& always-on PMF.*
Customer-led product strategy that starts with the customer's problem, validates the solution, and builds toward product-market fit. Product-market fit is an always-on discipline, not a milestone, because markets move and what was best-in-class today can be a commodity tomorrow.

## 02 :: Finance / CFO
### Financial fluency *& commercial alignment.*
P&L, balance sheet and cashflow as a product and commercial lens: pricing, contracts, billing, retention and go-to-market choices that the business can actually bear. A CFO who sits in the value stream, not the back office.

## 03 :: Operations / COO
### Operating-model design *& delivery.*
Org design and operating models for the 10-200+ growth-stage moment. Connected metrics across sales, product, delivery and finance, delivered through pragmatic, empathy-led transformation working groups, not directive cost-cutting.

## 04 :: Architecture / CTO
### Business-led solution *& architecture.*
Business-led solution architecture: ask whether the problem matters to the customer before choosing how to solve it. Technical enough to work credibly with engineering and data leaders. AI-native build capability.
